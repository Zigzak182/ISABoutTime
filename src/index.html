How we work

The tree is not the client. The site is.

A tree fails for one of three reasons: it was the wrong species for the site, it was never given structure when structure was cheap to build, or something happened to its roots that nobody saw. Almost every expensive tree problem is one of those three, decided years before the failure.

So we start below ground. We excavate the root flare, because girdling roots and deep planting cause more landscape tree decline than every insect and disease combined, and neither is visible from the canopy. We read the site — drainage, compaction, pH, salt, grade history, what the builder did. Then we look up.

What you get back is a written assessment with a rating, a time frame, and a set of options. Sometimes that is a removal quote. More often it is a $400 structural pruning that prevents a $6,000 crane job in twenty years, or a recommendation to move a parking spot instead of taking down a healthy oak.
