Before you write

Three details save an entire site visit:

  • Rough trunk diameter measured at about 4.5 ft above the ground.
  • What is underneath it — a driveway, a bedroom, a play set, a power line, nothing at all. This is the single biggest factor in whether a defect matters.
  • What changed recently — construction, a grade change, a new irrigation system, a storm, mulch piled against the trunk.

A photo of the whole tree plus a photo of the base is worth more than a paragraph of description.

If a limb is hanging or a tree is leaning into a target right now, do not wait on a form. Keep people out of the area and call directly.
