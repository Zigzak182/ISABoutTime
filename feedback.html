Feedback

Report an issue or contribute data

The species database and the ZIP-to-zone table are the parts most likely to be wrong in any given region, and the people best placed to correct them are the ones working there.

What's most useful

  • A species that's wrong for your region. Something listed as an asset that fails locally, or a liability call you'd argue with.
  • A missing cultivar. Especially named selections that solve a real problem — fruitless, male, narrow, disease-resistant.
  • A hardiness zone that's off. ZIP prefixes cover a lot of ground; mountain and coastal areas are the usual offenders.
  • A species missing entirely for a region the database covers thinly.
  • A bug — anything that miscalculates, fails to save, or breaks on your device.

Regional specifics beat general corrections. "Red maple is chlorotic on the limestone fill across most of this county" is actionable; "red maple is overrated" is not.

Field observation counts — just say so, and roughly how long.

Only used to weigh the correction. Nothing is published.

Nothing is transmitted from this page. The button opens your email client, or copies the report so you can paste it into a GitHub issue.

Contributing directly

          

            Seed is a static site with no build step and no dependencies. The data that drives
            the tools is three plain files you can edit and open in a browser:
          

          

            
data/species.js

              
79 taxa with cultivars, tolerances, and an asset and liability case for each.
                Every record carries a liability entry — a tree with none is a record nobody
                thought hard enough about.

            
data/zones.js

              
186 ZIP-prefix ranges mapped to hardiness zone, planting region and moisture
                regime. Splitting a range is the usual fix.

            
data/study.js

              
13 domains, 90 flashcards, 79 practice questions. Every question needs a real
                explanation — a bank that only says "wrong" teaches recognition, not material.

          

          

            docs/DATA.md documents each schema and includes validation
            commands to run after editing.
          

        


        

          Deploying your own copy? This form composes a
          mailto: message because the site is static and has no
          server. Set the destination in assets/js/feedback.js, or
          point it at an issue tracker. See docs/DEPLOY.md.
